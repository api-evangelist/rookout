OpenTelemetry standardizes how organizations collect traces, metrics, and logs. Dynatrace adds the topology and context needed to connect that telemetry across user experiences, services, databases, hosts, and AI workloads. This connected context means you can spend less time trying to understand your environment and more time on the work that drives business outcomes.
