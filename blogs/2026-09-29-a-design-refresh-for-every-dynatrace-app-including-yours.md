If Dynatrace looks different today, that’s intentional. The design refresh incorporates customer feedback to improve the user experience by reducing visual layering on page surfaces and improving contrast in dark mode. Status and chart colors are more vibrant and easier to tell apart, and charts have new themed palettes alongside the existing options.
