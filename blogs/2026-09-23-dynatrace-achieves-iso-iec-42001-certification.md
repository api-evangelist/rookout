Dynatrace has achieved the international standard certification for AI management systems, ISO/IEC 42001:2023. The certification applies to the AI management systems (AIMS) supporting the Dynatrace platform, including the AI-powered capabilities Dynatrace develops and embeds in Dynatrace SaaS and Dynatrace Managed.
